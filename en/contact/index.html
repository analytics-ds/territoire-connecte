Writing to the editorial team

Territoire Connecté welcomes factual corrections, topic suggestions and field feedback from local authorities.

Particularly useful:

  • Factual corrections on a published article, with the source that settles the point
  • Field feedback from authorities on a data platform, connected lighting or digitisation project
  • Open datasets that are little known and usable on the themes covered here

Before writing

For a correction, give the URL of the article concerned and the passage in question. For a topic suggestion, one paragraph is enough: the context, the question raised, and what is missing from what already exists.

Requests for sponsored articles and commercial link insertions do not receive a reply.
